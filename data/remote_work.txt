Remote Work Productivity Notes

Multiple large-scale studies since 2020 show mixed effects of remote work on
productivity, varying heavily by role type. Individual, focus-heavy tasks
(coding, writing, analysis) often show productivity gains due to fewer
interruptions and no commute.

Collaborative and creative work — brainstorming, onboarding new employees,
cross-team coordination — tends to suffer in fully remote settings compared
to in-person or hybrid arrangements, according to several workplace surveys.

Commercial real estate vacancy rates in major city downtowns rose sharply
following widespread adoption of hybrid and remote work policies.

Employee-reported job satisfaction and retention rates are generally higher
at companies offering remote or hybrid flexibility versus fully in-office
mandates, based on multiple HR industry reports.
