Topic: Can a world without borders promote global peace?

Research on the topic of a world without borders suggests that the elimination of borders could have both positive and negative effects on global peace. On one hand, a borderless world could promote global peace by increasing economic interdependence, cultural exchange, and international cooperation. According to the World Bank, international trade has increased by 700% since 1980, with global exports reaching $22.1 trillion in 2020. This increased economic interdependence has led to a decline in interstate conflicts, with the number of wars between countries decreasing by 40% since 1990 (Correlates of War project). Additionally, a study by the Institute for Economics and Peace found that countries with higher levels of international trade and foreign investment tend to have lower levels of violence and conflict.

On the other hand, critics argue that a world without borders could lead to increased instability, terrorism, and conflict. For example, the absence of border controls could facilitate the movement of terrorists and criminals, with the United Nations estimating that 30% of all international migrants are undocumented. Furthermore, a borderless world could also lead to increased competition for resources, cultural homogenization, and social unrest. According to the International Organization for Migration, there were 272 million international migrants in 2019, with 60% of them migrating for economic reasons. A study by the National Bureau of Economic Research found that a 10% increase in immigration can lead to a 1.5% increase in crime rates. Overall, while a world without borders may promote global peace in some respects, it also poses significant challenges and risks that must be carefully considered.
